# Nexora Technologies — IT Onboarding Guide

## Your First Day Setup

New employees receive a company laptop (MacBook Pro for Engineering/Product roles, or a Windows laptop for Operations/Client Success roles, depending on team standard) shipped to their home address or waiting at their office desk, arriving before their start date wherever possible.

Your Nexora email account (firstname.lastname@nexora.com) and Slack account are created automatically 2 business days before your start date. Login credentials are sent to your personal email on file; you'll be required to set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on first login.

## Required Accounts and Tools

All new employees need access to the following, provisioned automatically based on your department:

- **Slack** — primary internal communication tool for all teams
- **Google Workspace** — email, calendar, docs, and shared drives
- **Jira** — task and project tracking (Engineering and Product teams)
- **GitHub** — source code hosting (Engineering teams only); access is granted to specific repositories based on your team assignment
- **VPN Client** — required for accessing internal systems and production environments remotely; setup instructions are sent via email on your first day

## Requesting Additional Software

Any software or tool not automatically provisioned can be requested through the IT Service Desk portal. Requests under $50/month are typically auto-approved within 1 business day. Requests above that threshold require manager approval before IT will provision access.

## VPN and Remote Access

All access to internal systems, staging environments, and production infrastructure requires an active VPN connection, even when working from the office. VPN credentials are tied to your individual account and must not be shared. If your VPN connection fails, restart the VPN client first; if the issue persists after two attempts, submit a ticket to the IT Service Desk rather than troubleshooting extensively yourself, since VPN issues are often server-side and IT can diagnose them faster with direct access to logs.

## Password and Security Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and are required to be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts with access to client data or production systems. Sharing credentials with another employee, even temporarily, is a violation of company security policy regardless of intent.

## Reporting IT Issues

Non-urgent IT issues should be submitted through the IT Service Desk portal, with a typical response time of 1 business day. Urgent issues (systems down, security concerns, inability to access critical work tools) should be reported via the #it-urgent Slack channel, which is monitored during business hours across all three office time zones for faster coverage.

## Offboarding Note

For reference: when an employee departs the company, all system access is automatically revoked at end of business on their last working day, and company equipment must be returned within 5 business days.
