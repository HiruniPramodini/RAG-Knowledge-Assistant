# Aurelia Tech — Code of Conduct & Workplace Ethics

> **Notice:** This document contains illustrative demonstration conduct policies for *Aurelia Tech*. Last updated: January 2026. Document Owner: Legal, People Operations & Compliance (`compliance@aureliatech.com`).

---

## 1. Professional Conduct & Psychological Safety

Aurelia Tech is committed to fostering an inclusive, welcoming, and psychologically safe environment for all employees, contractors, partners, and clients. 

- **Constructive Collaboration:** Disagreements regarding architectural choices, technical designs, or business priorities are expected and healthy when conducted respectfully. Criticisms must focus objectively on systems, ideas, and code, never on individuals.
- **Inclusive Language:** Communication across Slack, email, documentation, code comments, and meetings must remain free of derogatory remarks, slurs, or exclusionary stereotypes.

---

## 2. Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination Policy

Aurelia enforces a strict **Zero-Tolerance Policy** against harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind.

- **Protected Attributes:** We prohibit unlawful discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neurodiversity, age, religion, marital status, or veteran status.
- **Scope of Coverage:** This policy applies to all workplace interactions, including remote communication channels (Slack, Google Meet, email), company-sponsored travel, social gatherings, and industry conferences where Aurelia is represented.
- **Microaggressions and Harassment:** Repeated unwanted behaviors, inappropriate sexual remarks, or offensive jokes will result in disciplinary intervention, up to and including immediate termination of employment.

---

## 3. Confidentiality, Client Privacy & Data Protection

As an enterprise software engineering company, Aurelia handles sensitive client data across regulated industries (healthcare, banking, retail).

- **Non-Disclosure Obligations:** Proprietary algorithms, trade secrets, unreleased client deliverables, client lists, and pricing models must remain strictly confidential during and after employment at Aurelia.
- **Handling Customer Data:**
  - Production customer data must **never** be copied to personal computers, external storage media, or unapproved cloud drives.
  - Production databases may only be accessed by authorized on-call engineers using just-in-time (JIT) access tokens with active audit logging.
  - Developers must use synthetic or anonymized mock data in local and staging environments.
- **Public Speaking & Social Media:** Employees are encouraged to participate in open source and developer conferences, but all presentations involving Aurelia case studies or proprietary architectures must be vetted by Legal and Communications prior to presentation.

---

## 4. Conflicts of Interest & Moonlighting

- **Outside Activities:** Outside consulting, advisory roles, directorships, or personal business ventures must not conflict with Aurelia's business or divert working hours and company resources.
- **Mandatory Disclosure:** Any potential conflict of interest—including personal financial investments in direct competitors or vendors—must be proactively disclosed to People Operations via the annual compliance declaration form.
- **Open Source Contributions:** Open-source contributions outside of company projects are supported, provided they do not incorporate Aurelia intellectual property or proprietary client code.

---

## 5. Acceptable Use of Company Systems & Equipment

- **Business Purpose:** Company-provided hardware (laptops, mobile devices) and software licenses are provided for business operations.
- **Incidental Personal Use:** Reasonable, non-disruptive personal use (such as reading personal news or personal email during scheduled breaks) is permissible.
- **Prohibited Activities:** It is strictly prohibited to use company systems to:
  - Download or distribute unlicensed, pirated, or copyrighted media.
  - Engage in cryptocurrency mining, unauthorized vulnerability probing, or illegal actions.
  - Bypass enterprise security controls, disable MDM agents, or bypass VPN firewalls.
- **Monitoring Disclosure:** To protect enterprise and client security, Aurelia reserves the legal right to audit, inspect, and monitor activity across company-owned devices, networks, and communication platforms.

---

## 6. Reporting Concerns & Whistleblower Protection

If you witness or suspect a breach of this Code of Conduct, financial fraud, security violations, or harassment, multiple reporting avenues are available:

1. **Direct Manager or Department Head:** For immediate team-level concerns.
2. **People Operations & HR:** Contact `people-ops@aureliatech.com` or `compliance@aureliatech.com`.
3. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline:** Operated by an independent third party, accessible 24/7 online at `https://ethics-hotline.aureliatech.com` or via toll-free telephone.
4. **Strict No-Retaliation Guarantee:** Aurelia strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against any team member who reports a violation in good faith. Retaliation is treated as a major disciplinary offense resulting in dismissal.

---

## 7. Disciplinary Actions & Remediation

Violations of this Code of Conduct are investigated impartially by People Operations and Legal. Depending on severity, consequences include:
- Documented written warning and performance improvement guidance.
- Mandatory re-training on workplace ethics and security.
- Reassignment or demotion.
- Immediate termination of employment for gross misconduct.
