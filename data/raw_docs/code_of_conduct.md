# Nexora Technologies — Code of Conduct

## Professional Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism, regardless of role, seniority, or department. Disagreements on technical or business decisions are normal and encouraged as part of healthy debate, but must remain focused on ideas rather than personal criticism.

## Anti-Harassment and Discrimination

Nexora maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. This applies to interactions in the office, remote communications (Slack, email, video calls), and company-sponsored events outside normal working hours.

## Confidentiality

Employees have access to confidential company information, including client data, financial information, and unreleased product plans, as part of their role. This information must not be shared outside the company, including on personal social media, without explicit written approval from the relevant department head. Confidentiality obligations continue after an employee's departure from the company, as outlined in individual employment agreements.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside business activities, board positions, or financial interests that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role at Nexora, particularly involving competitors, clients, or vendors. Disclosures are made confidentially to HR and reviewed on a case-by-case basis; having an outside interest does not automatically disqualify an employee, but failing to disclose one does violate policy.

## Use of Company Systems

Company laptops, accounts, and systems are provided for business use. Reasonable personal use (checking personal email, browsing during breaks) is permitted, but company systems must never be used to access or store illegal content, and employees should have no expectation of privacy regarding activity on company-owned devices or accounts, which may be monitored for security purposes.

## Reporting Violations

Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct should report it to HR directly, to their manager, or through the anonymous ethics hotline available on the internal HR portal. All reports are investigated, and retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is itself a serious violation of company policy, subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed on a case-by-case basis depending on severity, ranging from a documented verbal warning for minor first infractions to immediate termination for serious violations such as harassment, discrimination, or breach of confidentiality involving client data.
