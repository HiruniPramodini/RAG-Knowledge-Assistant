# Nexora Technologies — HR Policies

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for requests longer than 2 days, though shorter requests may be submitted with less notice at manager discretion. Unused leave may be carried over into the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days; any leave beyond that cap is forfeited unless local labor law requires otherwise.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year, which do not require advance notice but should be reported to your manager as soon as reasonably possible, ideally before the start of the workday. A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Nexora offers 16 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, applicable to birth, adoption, or fostering. Parental leave requests should be submitted to HR at least 30 days before the expected start date where possible.

## Working Hours and Flexibility

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM in the employee's local time zone, with a 30-minute lunch break. Nexora operates on a flexible schedule policy: employees may adjust their start and end times by up to 2 hours, provided they are available for core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM for team collaboration, and provided their manager is informed of the adjusted schedule.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely on a hybrid or fully remote basis, subject to role requirements and manager approval. Client Success and certain Engineering roles requiring in-person client presence may have minimum in-office day requirements, specified in individual role descriptions. Remote employees are expected to maintain reliable internet access and be available during core collaboration hours regardless of time zone flexibility.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business-related expenses (travel, client meals, approved software subscriptions, conference attendance) are reimbursable through the Finance portal. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Expense reports should be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred; reports submitted after 90 days may be declined. Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from your manager before being incurred, not after.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews occur twice yearly, in June and December. Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback from 2-3 colleagues selected by the employee and manager together. Compensation adjustments, when applicable, are typically communicated within 30 days of the December review cycle.

## Code of Conduct Reporting

Any concerns regarding workplace conduct, harassment, or policy violations can be reported confidentially to HR directly, or through the anonymous ethics hotline listed on the internal HR portal. Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action.
