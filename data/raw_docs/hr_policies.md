# Aurelia Tech — HR Policies & Employee Benefits

> **Notice:** This document contains illustrative demonstration policies for *Aurelia Tech*. Last updated: January 2026. Document Owner: People Operations / HR Department (`hr-support@aureliatech.com`).

---

## 1. Annual Leave & Vacation Policy

- **Annual Entitlement:** Full-time permanent employees accrue **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year (accrued at **1.67 days per full calendar month** worked).
- **Application & Approval:** Leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR Portal (*PeopleConnect*) at least **5 business days in advance** for requests exceeding 2 consecutive days. Shorter requests may be submitted with 24 hours' notice, subject to manager discretion.
- **Carryover Rules:** Employees may carry over up to **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be utilized within the first 6 months of the new year. Any accrued leave exceeding the 5-day rollover threshold is forfeited on December 31st, unless local statutory labor laws require otherwise.
- **Unpaid Leave:** Unpaid personal leave may be requested for up to 30 days after consulting People Operations and department head approval.

---

## 2. Sick Leave & Health Absences

- **Entitlement:** Employees are granted **10 paid sick days per calendar year**, available in full upon completion of the first month of employment.
- **Notification:** Sick leave does not require advance approval, but employees must notify their direct manager via Slack or email prior to or within two hours of their scheduled start time.
- **Medical Certification:** A physician's note or medical certificate is mandatory for any continuous sick leave lasting **longer than 3 consecutive business days**.
- **Mental Health Days:** Up to 2 of the 10 allocated sick days can be taken as personal mental health / restorative rest days without specific medical justification.

---

## 3. Parental Leave Policy

- **Primary Caregiver Leave:** Aurelia provides **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave** to the primary caregiver following the birth, legal adoption, or long-term foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
- **Eligibility & Notice:** Available to full-time employees after 6 months of continuous service. Notice should be submitted to HR via the portal at least **30 calendar days** prior to the anticipated start date.

---

## 4. Public Holidays & Floating Cultural Days

- **Observed Holidays:** Aurelia recognizes **11 official public holidays** annually, tailored to the employee's country of payroll (e.g., US, Canada, Portugal).
- **Floating Cultural Holidays:** Every employee receives **2 additional floating paid holidays** each year to observe personal religious, cultural, or commemorative occasions not covered by local statutory holidays.

---

## 5. Working Hours & Flexible Schedule

- **Standard Hours:** 40 hours per week, typically 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM in the employee's local time zone, including a 30-minute lunch break.
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All teams observe mandatory **Core Hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM** local time. Team members may flex their arrival and departure by up to 2 hours around core hours with manager alignment.

---

## 6. Remote Work & Home Office Policy

- **Remote-First Model:** Employees may work 100% remotely or on a hybrid schedule. 
- **Workspace Requirements:** Remote staff must maintain a secure, private working environment with high-speed internet (minimum 50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload).
- **Relocation Notice:** Moving to a different state or country requires 60 days advance notification to People Operations due to tax, payroll, and legal compliance obligations.

---

## 7. Employee Benefits Overview

1. **Healthcare, Dental & Vision:** Comprehensive insurance coverage starting on the first day of the calendar month following the hire date. Aurelia covers 85% of employee premiums and 65% of dependent premiums.
2. **Retirement Plan (401k / Pension):** Aurelia matches 100% of employee retirement contributions up to **4% of base salary**, vesting immediately.
3. **Learning & Development Stipend:** Every employee is allocated **$1,500 USD per year** for approved technical conferences, professional certifications, books, and university coursework.
4. **Home Office Equipment Stipend:** A one-time tax-free reimbursement of **$500 USD** is provided to new hires for ergonomic desk equipment, monitors, or office chairs.
5. **Wellness Benefit:** A monthly stipend of **$75 USD** for gym memberships, fitness classes, mindfulness apps, or ergonomic wellness programs.

---

## 8. Probation Period & Performance Reviews

- **Probation Period:** New full-time hires participate in a **90-day onboarding and probation period**. Structured progress milestones occur at **Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90** with the hiring manager.
- **Bi-Annual Performance Reviews:** Formal performance reviews take place in **June (Mid-Year Reflection)** and **December (Year-End Evaluation)**. Reviews involve self-evaluations, manager reviews, and 360-degree peer feedback. Merit salary adjustments and promotions are finalized within 30 days after the December review.

---

## 9. Expense Reimbursement Process

- **Eligible Expenses:** Business travel, approved software licenses, client entertainment, and home office supplies.
- **Receipt Threshold:** Itemized receipts are required for all expenses exceeding **$25 USD**.
- **Pre-Approval Requirement:** Any individual expense exceeding **$500 USD** must be pre-approved in writing by the department manager before incurring the cost.
- **Submission Timeline:** Submit expense claims through the Finance Portal within **30 days** of the transaction. Claims submitted after 90 days cannot be processed.

---

## 10. HR Escalation & Support Contacts

- **General Inquiries:** `hr-support@aureliatech.com` (SLA: 24 business hours).
- **Benefits Administration:** `benefits@aureliatech.com`.
- **Anonymous Ethics & Conduct Hotline:** Accessible 24/7 via phone or web portal on the internal intranet. Retaliation against any employee reporting in good faith is strictly prohibited.
